Building a shared Look out of values and effects

Busk it, then name it. The values and the effect are in two different places while you work — Local, and the programmer’s effect band — and Record is what gathers both into one Look.

Path 1 — busk first (recommended)

1

Focus Local. Select your heads and set the values — colour, level, position.

2

Add the effect. With Local focused it lands in the programmer band, not in any Look — nothing to name yet.

3

Record → A new Look. Tick the values and the effects that belong in it. →

4

The Look is created and immediately becomes a layer at the top of your stack. What you ticked leaves Local; what you didn’t stays yours.

Path 2 — declare first

+ Look → New empty puts an empty Look in the stack. Focus it, and every value and effect you author goes straight in, because the scope is the Look. No gathering step.

Better when you already know it is shared and want to see it composited against the layers beneath as you build. Worse when you are still finding the look on the rig — which is most of the time, hence the default.

Two things that do not travel

Delay and interval. LookEffect has no timing fields — timing belongs to the layer. A busked effect’s “fire after 3s” becomes the layer’s delay, so it is per-use, not baked into the Look.

Your local overrides. If you busked a colour on top of a layer that was already asserting one, only your value records — the sheet counts what Local holds, not the cook.
