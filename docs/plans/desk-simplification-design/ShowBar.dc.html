The show bar as it narrows

Brief item 7. The bar collapses on its own @container width, not the viewport — correct, since the sidebar insets the content region. What goes wrong is what it does when it runs out of room: it deletes information.

Two thresholds fire at 560px in opposite directions

SpeedMastersStrip hides its tile below @[560px]. The live-state block appears above @[560px]. So in the 560–900px band both are on screen at once, alongside DBO, M1 + TAP, the programmer tile, and BACK + GO at min-w-[92px] — roughly 470px of shrink-0. The live block is flex-1 min-w-0, so it takes the whole shortfall and the cue number clips while nominally “visible”.

Two more, from the same family: ProgrammerIndicator’s @[760px] queries resolve against whatever container it lands in — in RunMobile there is none, so they would never match. And useNarrowContainer defaults to narrow = true, so every mount paints the phone bar for one frame.
