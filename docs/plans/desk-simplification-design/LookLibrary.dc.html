The row previews like a cue row

Swatches for colour, bars for level — the same compressed strip the Show view draws on a cue. A Look and a cue are the same kind of thing at different scales, so they should read alike here too.

No family filter here

A Look spans families by nature, so filtering by one would hide most of them from most filters. The filter belongs on /templates, where a family is an exact partition. Row menu still carries Edit in programmer, Duplicate, Copy to project, Delete.
