Timeline mode of Act 1, show running and unlocked. Q9 is live and fading in; Q10 is next. Lavender on LX1 Orbits is selected in Q2 to Q4.
1Timeline is a third mode beside Cards and Table, at /show/stacks/:id/timeline. It keeps the Table's rows, row drag, edit lock and bottom pane (D2).
2Row B filters. The kind chips hide whole groups. The family filter keeps palette, effect and Look columns that touch a family. Selection only keeps columns whose targets meet the desk selection (D11).
3A cell is that cue's own layer (D4). The small number is its rank in the cue, 1 at the bottom. Glyphs mark non-default settings: S stomp, 60% amount, C a Colour-only mask, ⏱ a timed layer, off disabled.
4Q9 is live: a green edge and a fade bar along the row's foot, as the show bar's tile draws it. Edited is show-cue-editing's D4: the cue changed since its GO, and selecting the row offers Update stage….
5The selection bar names the column and the range. Verbs apply per cell: On is greyed because every cell is already on. Replace with… and Settings… are the two popovers on the Editing board.
6One effect run, Q2 to Q6, sharing a run key (D7). At Q4 the Pulse slows to one beat: the GO edits it in place with its phase kept, marked ◆ (D8).
7Two keys meet at Q6/Q7: a dotted break and ↺. Colour Cycle restarts at Q7's GO. This is how effects copied between cues before the run key read: Join run fixes it.
8Scenery: a change is a chip, and a thin line carries the state down until the next change (D9). The house tabs hold Drawn from Q1 to the end; SB Scrim tracks Closed from Q0 through the marker.
9The pane follows one selected cell to its cue, on the column's tab (call C2). A range over several cues folds it, as the Table does for several rows.
