Colour chases on colour-wheel heads

Today a colour FX skips every head whose colour is a wheel. That covers the Beam Rings, the MAC 250s and the Robes. This proposal lets Colour Cycle and Palette Steps chase those heads too, each step on the head's nearest slot, and adds Split: a step through the half-and-half position between two neighbouring slots, on the wheels that have one.

How I read the ask. "FXs that use gobos" is taken to mean fixtures with a gobo wheel. Those spots and beams all make their colour with a wheel. A gobo chase on them already works, through Slot Steps on the gobo wheel. If you meant a colour chase locked to a gobo chase, that is a smaller change, and the playground's timing still applies.

Today

Where a colour chase stops

  • Colour FX skip wheel heads. ColourTarget takes only a head with a mixed colour, so a Beam Ring in the selection is left out with no message. FU-FX-STEPS-COLOUR-WHEEL records this for Palette Steps, and this request meets its trigger.
  • Slot Steps is the only wheel chase. It works by slot name and is filed under Beam. Names differ between models (the Varytec's COLOR_1…7, the Fusion's RED), so one list can't cover a mixed rig, and it can't include an RGB par.
  • The snap already exists. A colour palette pressed on a wheel head lands on the slot nearest by ΔE, against each slot's preview or the unit's fitted gel. A chase can use the same rule.
  • Splits aren't modelled. The MAC 250 documents split positions at 1–144 but doesn't list them, and the Robe's wheels scroll between neighbours over 0–127. The Stage view would draw the MAC 250 at level 70 as open white.

The rule

What each kind of head does

A wheel can't fade, so it changes when the RGB heads are half-way across, and the rig changes on one beat. Split is new. It holds the half-and-half between two neighbouring slots for Fade of each step.

BetweenRGB headWheel that splits, neighboursAny other wheel
CutA, then B at the step's edgeA, then B at the step's edgeSame
Fade · SmoothA, crossing to BA, then B at the middle of the crossingSame
Split newA, the half-way mix held, then BA, then the A/B split held, then BA, then B at the middle of the split

Only FX that step reach a wheel head: Colour Cycle and Palette Steps. Rainbow Cycle, Colour Flicker, Colour Strobe and script FX still leave a wheel alone, since snapping a continuous colour every tick would rattle the wheel. Under Amount a wheel still snaps at half weight, as every setting does.

1

Start from the wheel. On a selection whose wheel heads are one model, the picker starts Colour Cycle on that wheel's colours in wheel order, open left out and fitted gels read. Every neighbour in the list is then a neighbour on the wheel. Split Chase is the same thing as a preset.

2

Split is a fourth option of between, reusing Fade as the share of each step held on the split. At 50 % that gives colour, split, colour, split, the classic half-step wheel chase.

3

The desk says what each head will do. The FX preview answers each wheel head's slot, or slot and split, and the notes: two colours on one slot, a split that can't happen, a step shorter than the wheel's travel. The editor draws them and works none of them out.

Decisions

Nine decisions

#DecisionWhere
D1A colour chase reaches wheel heads. A colour FX that declares stepsFrom counts a wheel head as a member and lands each step on its nearest slot, through the same snap and fitted media a palette press uses. One FX covers pars and spots, and every head counts toward Order and Spread.desk
D2Colour Cycle returns steps. It hands the target its colour list, not a colour it blended itself, so each head can snap. RGB output stays identical to the DMX step, held by an equivalence test against today's script.desk
D3A wheel changes at the middle of the crossing under Fade and Smooth, and at the edge under Cut.desk
D4between gains Split. Held for Fade of each step. A wheel that can split shows the split, any other wheel changes at its middle, and an RGB head holds the half-way mix.both
D5A wheel declares where it splits. Each colour slot can declare its level in the wheel's continuous band. Two slots split only if both declare one and they are neighbours, and their split is the midpoint. A test keeps the levels in wheel order. Nothing is guessed from names.desk
D6Slot Steps on a colour wheel takes Split too, since slot names on one model's wheel fix the neighbours exactly.desk
D7The picker starts from the wheel, in wheel order, on Split where the wheel splits and Cut where it doesn't. A Split Chase preset.client
D8The preview says what each head will do: its slot or split per sample, and notes for two colours on one slot, a split that can't happen and a step faster than the wheel's travel.both
D9The Stage view and tables draw a split: a half-and-half disc in 2D, the share-weighted beam and a pool split in two in 3D, and Red / Yellow in the fixtures table. A shared vector pins the split reading on both sides.client

No stored change. Split is a new value of an existing FX parameter, and an older desk runs it as Fade. Wheel membership and split levels live in fixture code. No table, migration or sync format moves.

Answered 23 Nov 2026

Four questions

  1. Under Split, do RGB heads hold the half-way mix or cut?

    Holding the mix keeps one rhythm across the rig, since every head changes at the same moments. Cutting keeps every par on a colour that is in the list.

    agreed Hold the mix.

  2. Under Fade, does a wheel change at the middle or roll?

    Rolling moves the wheel through the continuous band and shows every slot between the two. That's real wheel behaviour, but rarely the colour asked for.

    agreed Change at the middle. Rolling is out of scope.

  3. Should Rainbow Cycle drive a wheel's own rotation?

    A wheel head could run its scroll band under a Rainbow Cycle rather than being left alone.

    agreed Out of scope, filed as FU-FX-RAINBOW-WHEEL-SCROLL.

  4. Does the Beam Ring split?

    Your example chases from session 1. Whether it splits depended on what its 16-step bands hold.

    answered No, and nor do the Fusion or the Varytec. A forked Colour Cycle gets a line in the fork sheet saying it needs this desk or later.

Three sessions

Session 1 · desk

Wheel heads in colour chases

D1–D3. Colour Cycle on steps with its equivalence test. The colour target finds where each head lands, and suppression, Blind, the key stack, provenance and park ask it, because a wheel head's chase writes colourWheel, not rgbColour. Palette Steps reaches wheel heads, which closes FU-FX-STEPS-COLOUR-WHEEL.

Session 2 · desk

Splits

D4–D6 and D8. Split levels on the colour slots and their test, the MAC 250 and Robe annotated as estimates, the split write in both targets, Slot Steps' Split, and the preview's slots, splits and notes.

Session 3 · client

Editor and Stage view

D7 and D9. The Split option and wheel start, the Split Chase preset, the preview's notes in the inspector, the split drawn in 2D, 3D and the fixtures table, the shared vector, and manual-validation rows per split fixture.
