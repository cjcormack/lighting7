# Wheel colour chases: design record

Source: a design session on 2026-11-23. Chris asked for a colour chase on the fixtures with gobos,
the Show-Sound LEDBEAMRING his example, taking into account split colours on fixtures that support
them. Those heads all make their colour with a wheel, and today every colour FX skips them.

The page is [`wheel-colour-chase.html`](wheel-colour-chase.html). The engineering half is
[`../wheel-colour-chase-plan.md`](../wheel-colour-chase-plan.md). Where the plan and the page
disagree, the plan wins on behaviour and the page on layout and copy.

## Asked and answered

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Under Split, do RGB heads hold the half-way mix or cut? | **Hold the half-way mix**, so the rig changes on one beat. Cutting at the middle, and fading across the split, were declined. |
| Under Fade or Smooth, does a wheel change at the middle or roll? | **Change at the middle.** Rolling through the band, always or only between neighbours, was declined. |
| Should Rainbow Cycle drive a wheel's own rotation? | **Out of scope**, filed as a follow-up. |
| Does the Beam Ring split? | **No.** Nor do the Fusion 100 Spot or the Varytec XL60. Only the MAC 250 and the Robe ColorSpot 575 split. |
| How is a forked Colour Cycle's dependence on the new desk handled? | **A line in the fork sheet.** Doing nothing, and refusing to fork Colour Cycle, were declined. |
