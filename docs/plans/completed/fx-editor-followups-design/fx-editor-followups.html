FX Editor Follow-ups

Six things found using the FX editor on the rig, and what changes for each. Five are the editor's own drawing and wording; one, a fixture's elements running as one, is a gap in the desk's model that MagicQ fills with its Dup Elements.

  • The ruler numbers bars and beats from the master's tick, as the hover's Beat does, and holds the Cycle tab at the first cycle's end. Below a mouse it is a read-out; handles are pointer-only, as now.
  • Handles and marks are told apart by shape: a knob is a handle, a tick or a thin line is a mark. Hovering a knob names it (Spread 100%) and lights what it changes, the inspector's hover rule run the other way.
  • The toolbar is one row of the shell's 28px segments, each labelled, and the window's length (8 beats · 4 s) sits beside Window, where it was mistaken for a control.
  • The mix is drawing, not output. The client averages the desk's own samples for a row the desk does not send, as As seen already multiplies two of them. A level averages; a colour averages in RGB.
  • Folding is per window, beside the folded lanes. A folded fixture draws its mixed row alone, so a rig of 12-cell bars reads as bars.
  • Over takes the Fan's word for the same question (Over: Heads · Elements), and is drawn only where a selected fixture has elements.

D3 · Steps that land on a sample

Colour Cycle picks its colour with (phase / segmentSize).toInt(). At a third of a cycle that division is sometimes 0.9999… and sometimes 1.0, depending on the row's offset, so a step lands a sample late on one row and early on the next. Read off the screenshot at 16.7px a sample:

  • Colour Cycle, Palette Steps, Slot Steps and the square and strobe waves share a step function on the desk that adds a tolerance before it floors, so a boundary at an exact fraction lands on that sample in every row.
  • The sampler test that pins it runs Colour Cycle over three elements and asserts every step is 16 samples wide; it fails today.
  • On stage the error is one tick (a 24th of a beat), never seen; the graph draws it a sample wide, which is why it showed there first.

  • The stored default does not move. Canonical JSON omits a field at its default, so an archive's FX with no element mode means PER_FIXTURE forever. D8 is the creation doors (the picker, + FX, the palette editor, the cue editor, MCP's add tools) writing WHOLE explicitly where the attribute lives only on elements.
  • Sync: a desk that does not know WHOLE cannot read an FX that carries it, so the archive's formatVersion and minReader both move.
  • Glossary: Per Fixture and Flat become Each fixture and Elements on screen; Fixtures is new; Over is the control's word.
  • What item 6 asked for, with the Shehds: Over Fixtures, Colour Cycle with one colour per fixture, a cycle as many beats as fixtures, Spread 100%. Each beat the colours move one fixture; Fade crossfades them.

Not changing

  • The graph is still the desk's samples; nothing here computes an FX's output on the client.
  • Shapes stay the nine. Over is not a Shape: it decides what the Shapes count.
  • Handles stay pointer-only and stay off read-only and coarse-pointer hosts.
