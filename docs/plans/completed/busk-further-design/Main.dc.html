1The rig is built, not listed. Named rows of the targets the operator chose, in their order; hidden fixtures are simply not on it. Empty rig = every group then every fixture, as today.
2Focus is this window's. Split · Pads · Rig, per tab like the page chip, launchable as ?focus=. Pads or Rig fills the body; the other folds to a strip. Revised 2026-09-21: the band has a controls row under its label row — the Cells menu and steps, the verbs, then the Focus control and Edit layout — the same row in every shape, Pads included (the band folded to its two rows and the grip); the page strip is tabs and the page chip only, so no control moves as the shape changes.
3The split is in rows. Drag the handle and the band shows 1…N whole rows; a half row of tiles is useless, so it snaps — past the last row into Rig, above the first into Pads. Revised 2026-09-21: the handle is a bare grip with no count and no badge: while it is held the clip is the snap — the rows cut to nothing in the Pads region, every line shown in the Rig region — and in Pads and Rig a chevron pill sits where the grip would be, a press on it returning to Split. The rows carry a width share and a flow (Scroll · Wrap · Column) as banks do, so two half-width rows share a line and the handle counts lines.
4One side sheet, three tabs, one fold. Speed as today; Colour writes literals to the selection; Spread fans a value across it. Which tab and whether it is open is this window's.
5Spread… is a selection verb beside Locate and Highlight: it opens the Spread tab pre-aimed at the selection.
6Cells are on the tile. A multi-head fixture shows its cells; tap the tile for the whole, a pip for a cell; a selected pip is the accent, solid. Revised 2026-09-21: the sub-selection is one Cells menu holding the seven filters (All · Odd · Even · 1st half · 2nd half · Invert · Masters only), and Prev / Next are two step buttons beside it — a step moves the selection where a filter narrows it.
