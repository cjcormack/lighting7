One press, whatever you press it from

On the first evening in the hall, putting a palette, a Look or an effect onto some fixtures went through seven different mechanisms. Each picked its fixtures differently, honoured a different mask and landed somewhere different. This plan makes them one press. It takes a source and the window's selection (fixtures and attributes), and lands where the programmer is pointed. Every cell's popup offers that family's palettes.

The engineering plan is docs/plans/completed/apply-model-plan.md, and this page is its one-page copy, checked in as docs/plans/completed/apply-model-design/apply-model.html. Where they differ, the markdown wins. "Palette" is venue-visit D2's word for what the code still calls a template.

Decisions§2 · agreed 2026-10-15

D1A palette press puts on a layer that tracks it, on every surface. Values come only from Apply as values or a layer's Detach.
D2One palette per head per family. Pressing one on heads that hold another of its family takes them off it. Pressing it on heads it covers takes it off them.
D3One mask, the selection's. The family pill is its picker. Record, Record Look, Include, Make layer and Update open on it.
D4The popup puts bands round the editor: the family's palettes above, effects below, All … for the rest.
D5A bare effect is Local on the whole selection, and edits follow the selection. With a subset selected, those heads split off at their phase (MagicQ's rule).
D6Value palettes replace value palettes, effect palettes replace effect palettes, and Looks stack. Amber Breathe can run over Red.
D7With a Look focused, a press goes into the Look: a palette's values into its rows. In R2's cue editor a palette goes on as a cue layer.
D8⌥click is Apply as values; a hold opens the menu, on every surface.
D9The MCP tools follow the UI: apply_palette layers by default, asValues for literals.
D10The busk side sheet gets a tab per family: Level, Colour, Position, Beam, each the popup's body.

The plan adds its own rules. The desk owns every rule and the client keeps no copy. A press widens a record's layer only when that layer is on top. Solo banks keep their meaning. A palette layer's effect is edited as today. Include stays its own verb. Nothing is stored differently: no table, no column, no formatVersion.

The model§3

A press is a source, onto a selection, into a destination. Each of the three has one answer, read in one place, so every door is the same press.

sourceA palette, a Look or an effectA palette (a value or an effect palette) or a Look is a named record. A bare effect from the library is not. A cue pad is playback, outside the model.
selectionFixtures × attributes, the window'sThe desk selection's targets and its mask, or the window's own while it is unlinked. Every door reads the same pair. No door picks its own fixture.
destinationWhere the programmer is pointedThe programmer, unless a record is open for editing: a focused Look layer takes the press into that Look, and R2's cue editor into that cue.
rule 1 · agreedNamed things track; direct things are LocalA palette or Look goes on as a layer that follows the record. Values you set, a spread, an aim and a bare effect go into Local. Detach turns a layer into Local; Make layer and Save as palette go the other way.
rule 2 · agreedOne palette per head per familyPressing Blue on heads that hold Red takes those heads off Red. Value and effect palettes are kept apart, and Looks stack. The ring then always means "this is on stage", and pressing again turns it off.
rule 3 · existing, now everywhereThe mask cuts, the layer keeps the cutA palette outside the mask is refused. A Look lands with mask ∩ its families and names what it skipped. A cell's popup is masked to its own family.
