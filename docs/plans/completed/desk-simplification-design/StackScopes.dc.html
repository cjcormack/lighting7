Editing a Look edits it everywhere

This is the cost of composing in place, and it must be said at the moment of the edit, not in a tooltip. The first change to a Look used by other cues asks once:

Change all 9 layers Duplicate for this cue Cancel

Duplicate is the primary: retuning one cue is the common intent, and the destructive reading of “just edit it” is the one an operator cannot undo across nine cues. A Look used by nothing else skips the question entirely.

Reordering mid-session must not restart phase

Dragging a layer while a chase is running has to keep the chase where it is. The programmer stack already gets this right — ProgrammerLookStack deliberately does not renumber sortOrder client-side the way the cue path does, because the server renumbers and re-ranks the running effects in place.

Which matters more now than it did: with composition happening live, a drag is a normal authoring gesture rather than a rare one. Any new reorder affordance — keyboard, the Output-cell jump, promote-into-position — has to route through the same op.
