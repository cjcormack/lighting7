How the row foldsThe band is its own @container; the numbers are measured on this board and the app re-measures its own. Worded, the controls are ~1000px; iconic ~770; with the Cells and Focus words gone ~630; the summary wants ~150. In Pads the verbs fold first — icons below @[1180px] (the band carries data-focus, so the query can say so) — because there the words matter less than what is selected; in Split and Rig they hold their words to @[1020px]. The Cells and Focus words go at @[820px] in every shape — the Cells control keeps its mode word (All · Odd · Even · 1st · 2nd · Invert · Masters) and drops only the Cells: prefix, so it never becomes a bare glyph — the summary truncates below that, and below @[600px] the row becomes two rows by design: the selection verbs on the first, the chip, the summary and the Focus control on the second.

1One row, same order in every shape, and the chip never moves. RIG label, the Cells menu and its two steps, the four verbs, then the family pill and the desk chip left-anchored right after the verbs, then the gap, then the Focus control and Edit layout right-anchored. The chip sits at the same x in Split, Rig and Pads; only what follows it changes. (A first draft centred the pill and chip in the spare width, so they drifted between shapes.) The band is 32px shorter in all three.
2The summary is drawn only in Pads. In Split and Rig the lit tiles say it — Front wash on, Bar L · 4 of 8 — and the fold strip's head count is one more copy. In Pads there are no tiles, so Front wash, Bar L · 14 heads sits in the gap, min-w-0 with an ellipsis, and is the first thing to give when the row is short.
3The family pill stays beside the chip, and is drawn only while a mask is set. It is the selection's attribute mask — the families half of the desk selection, which a marquee over colour cells on the programmer publishes as Colour — and the tiles cannot show it. It is absent for a plain selection, so most of the time the chip follows the verbs directly; when it is there, a template outside it is refused by name and a Look's rows outside it are skipped, which is why it earns its 18px. If it should go too, it is one span.
4The folds are re-measured, Pads folds its verbs first, and the floor is two rows. The controls row measured 847px worded in the app; the pill, the chip and their gaps add ~170, and Pads wants ~150 more for the summary. So the verbs fold at ~1020 in Split and Rig and at ~1180 in Pads (the band carries data-focus), the Cells and Focus words at ~820 everywhere, and the summary is the next to give. The Cells control folds to its mode word, not a glyph: All is the state an operator most needs to be sure of, and Odd or Masters on a 24px button is what the menu was for. Below ~600 the band takes two rows by decision (Chris, 2026-09-21), not by flex-wrap: the verbs on the first, the chip, the summary and Focus on the second, so nothing lands mid-row. The short board's merged 32px strip and the narrow board's row chip are unchanged.
