Separating the venue from the show

A venue is the hall as hung: room, tabs, bars, house patch, universes. It is versioned; a show pins one version and layers its own kit, overrides and hides on top. Seating is fixed or movable per block. Five boards show how it looks and works, from the Projects page to opening a show the hall has moved on from.
