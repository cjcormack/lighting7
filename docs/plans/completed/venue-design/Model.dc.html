Versions and pins
The show's rig = venue at its pinned version ⊕ the show's layer

Additions
Patches, riggings, elements, groups and viewpoints the show owns — hires, booms, the set, movable seating.

Facet overrides
One row per (venue record uuid, facet): Address, Position, Focus, Media. A whole facet or nothing.

Hides
A venue fixture, rigging or element this show doesn't use. The venue keeps it.

Decisions
D1A venue is a project of kind VENUE. A show references one — venue uuid + version — or none. Every existing table keeps its project FK; extracting a venue moves rows between projects with their uuids. D2Pinned, not live. A venue change reaches a show only through Update. D3Explicit publish from one draft, with a note. Versions are immutable. Admins only. D4Overrides are per facet: Address, Position, Focus, Media. Identity (name, key, type, head) is the venue's. D5The show layer is additions, facet overrides and hides, over one key namespace. D6Seating is per block. Fixed blocks (the balcony) are the venue's, with their seat views. Preset blocks are copied into a show at creation or by Replace from preset. D7Output-affecting drift opens in Blind; drawing-only drift is a banner. Run against vN is per session; the installed version's trigger and command channels stay guarded. D8A venue syncs as its own repo. A version is a commit tagged v5; a show's project.json carries venueUuid + venueVersion, and importing one whose venue is absent asks for the venue repo. D9No touring tool. Detach flattens venue ⊕ show into a standalone project. D10The hall owns everything patched today, movers, LED kit and cannon included. A show adds what it brings in, like the panto's hires. D11The Programmer works in a venue for testing and focus; Record is off. D12Venues have groups (FOH, LX1, House, Movers). A show adds its own, which may hold venue fixtures; a venue group never holds a show's.

DB uuids aren't unique-indexed, so snapshots share them. show_overrides · show_hidesPortable with the show: record table + uuid, facet, value. Canonical JSON; formatVersion 24. uuid links firstGroup members, busk rig tiles, cue events and scenery rows point at patches and elements by row id. Considered and declined

Live inheritance. A venue fix would reach every show at once, including archived ones whose addresses then drive the wrong kit.

A version per save. The list fills with noise and every show is always behind.

The venue copied into each show's repo. Self-contained, but two sources of truth for one hall.

Per-field overrides. A shutter cut without the position it was cut against is meaningless; facets keep related fields together.

One seating switch per venue (fixed or per show). The Commemoration Hall is both: a fixed balcony over movable stalls.

Separate venue tables re-pointed from project to venue: a non-additive change to every table, with no migrations.

Settled in the second round

The hall's kit. Every fixture patched in project 9 today is the hall's (D10). Shows other than the panto are example names.

Seating per block, not one switch per venue: the hall itself is mixed, a fixed balcony over movable stalls (D6).

Programmer and groups in a venue, as drawn (D11, D12).

The four facets stand: Address · Position (rigging, pose, extra placements) · Focus (lantern, zoom, shutters, iris, softness) · Media (gel, gobos).
