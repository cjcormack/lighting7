Programmer

You set it. Filled once touched since the last Include; an outline while it is only what Include brought in. A Look you pressed in the programmer names itself: Night · pressed.

Pulse · ¼ · M2

An effect is moving it — its name, division and master. The slider and the pad follow the live value, in violet.

Q12 · Night

A cue holds it: the cue's number, and the Look layer's name when a layer won it, as the grid's hover says today.

Parked

Loud, because edits do nothing: the row's controls are disabled and its stack offers Unpark.

Base

Nothing asserts it. The row is drawn at 62% opacity; its controls stay live, since setting it is the commonest gesture.

Staged · blind

Blind: your value is held but not on stage. The chip names what is on stage; the field shows what leaving Blind would land.

Programmer

Held back: something under the winner is not running on stage because of it — almost always an effect under your value.

Programmer · 4 of 6

Mixed, on a group or several heads: the members disagree on the source; the stack lists each source with its heads.

1
The chip needs nothing new.

provenanceState already names the winner per (target, property) — park, programmer, effect or cue, with the cue, the layer and the effect — and getKeyState joins the programmer's entry. The sheet subscribes per row as the grid does.

2
The stack needs one read from the desk.

What sits under the winner is not on the wire: a held-back effect never appears in provenance, and the cue's value underneath is only resolved for Update (ProvenanceService.underlyingSources). Proposed: programmer.keyStack {target, property}, answered on request, never broadcast — Model board.

3
The held-back dot is cheap.

The client already has the effect list and the programmer's entries; an effect on a held (fixture, property) that is not a programmer effect is held back — the rule FxSheet's strike-through already draws. The dot comes from that; the stack's wording from the desk.

4
A stack is the popover form of the editor kit's surface.

EditorSurface: a popover beside the row on a desk, a bottom sheet on a phone, a side sheet on a short viewport. Clear in it is the row's ×; Unpark is the header's.
