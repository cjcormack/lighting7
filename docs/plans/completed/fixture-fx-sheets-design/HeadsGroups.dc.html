Fixture and FX sheets · heads and groups

A strip picks the heads, the rows below edit them — for a bar's cells and a group's members alike

Today a 12-head bar is twelve accordions under the fixture's own controls, and a group is a different sheet with different visualisers that never writes a group. Both become the one sheet with a strip under the header: All, or any run of pips, drawn in their live colours — the busk rig tile's pips, grown to a row.

All writes the group — one entry carrying the group, which Record turns back into a group row — and starts group effects with a distribution. Members is today's member grid; a member opens its own sheet, where a group's effect shows via Front wash.

1
The strip is the sheet's own pick, not the desk selection.

Picking heads on a sheet does not move every other screen's targets; the sheet's own count says what a write lands on. Locate and Highlight act on the pick.

2
A pick is a run or a set, the busk pip's rules: a tap toggles, a mouse drag runs, a held finger runs. All is one press back. A pip shows its head's live colour from the stage's colour dispatch, as a rig tile does.

3
On a group, a subset writes members.

With All the sheet writes one group entry; with a subset it writes those members, each carrying the group as its source, so Record can still tell. The sheet never sends a raw channel for a group, which the group sheet's sliders do today.

4
Mixed is a range and a dashed edge.

A slider over several heads draws the range of their values and a thumb at the highest, as the group visualisers do; a colour draws its heads as a strip. The chip counts how many heads its source holds (4 of 6), and the stack lists each source with its heads.

5
Effects follow the pick.

+ Effect on a pick of heads targets those cells; on a group's All it targets the group, with Distribution and Element mode in the editor's Advanced. The chip names the target: Rainbow · heads, Colour fade · linear.

6
What goes.

ElementsView's accordions and aggregate preview, GroupPropertyVisualizers and GroupDetailModal's separate body, the "Virtual" badges (the all-heads properties are the strip's All), and the fixture's virtual dimmer, which becomes the master row only where a head has no dimmer of its own.
