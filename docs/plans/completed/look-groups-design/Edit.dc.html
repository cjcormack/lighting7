Editing happens in place. The page you busk is the page you edit: rename a bank in its header, flip Solo, cross a pad off, drag pads within or between banks or in from the library, drag a bank beside a column, under another bank, or below the page. Every gesture saves; Done only leaves the mode. Pads do not press while editing, and the target band dims to say so.

The library is a palette. While editing it takes the speed rail's place, a little wider: every template, Look and cue under one search and two filters, each row a pad waiting to be dragged onto a bank. The drop slot opens where it will land; a record already on the page says so and may still be placed again. No ticking, no confirm, nothing to add "to".
