+ Bank

The overlay stays what it is. Eight slots a page, dots and a swipe between pages, a tap toggles, tiles light from the stack's live cue or the Look's presence. No banks, no solo, no selection: it holds only what needs none — cues, and Looks bound to their fixtures. A Look with a deferred effect cannot land here; the palette says needs a selection and no slot opens for it.

Its members come from the busk view's library. While the busk view is in edit mode the slots grow their crosses and become drop targets for the same palette rows the banks take; swapping two slots by drag is as today. Its own long-press wiggle mode and inline assign panel go. Outside edit mode it is exactly the panel that exists now.
