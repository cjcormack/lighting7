The layout, the press, and the delta

The busk page becomes a thing the operator builds. It references library records, it never copies them, and it owns the one fact a template group used to own: what else goes off when a pad is pressed.

The layout
Page

A name and a position. A project has any number; the first is generated from the library on first open, or left empty.

Bank

A name, solo (off means stack) and a flow: pads wrap to the bank's width, or run as one column. It sits in a column of a row: a page is rows, a row is columns each with a width share, a column stacks banks top to bottom. No coordinates, so nothing overlaps; columns stack on a narrow screen.

Pad

An ordered reference: {kind: TEMPLATE | LOOK | CUE, uuid}. One record may sit on several pads. Deleting the record deletes its pads — a pad is an enrichment, never a guard.

Tables

busk_pages, busk_columns (page, row, position, width), busk_banks, busk_pads — the pad with three nullable FKs and exactly one set, the DaoCueLayers pattern. Rows are the column's row number, renumbered densely by the layout route. Portable: one canonical document per page under buskPages/, banks and pads nested, references by uuid; a dangling reference drops the pad and logs.

Routes

GET /projects/{id}/busk (every page); pages POST / PUT / DELETE / reorder; and PUT /busk/pages/{pid}/layout — the whole page: rows of columns of banks with pads, every bank named once, pads without an id created. One keyed frame, buskLayoutChanged.

A press
POST /busk/pads/{padId}/press

Body: the selection. The server reads the pad, its record and — when the bank is solo — the records on its sibling pads, in one transaction, as the template toggle reads its group today.

By kind

Template → toggle with its family mask and the siblings. Look → toggle with no mask and the siblings. Cue → apply / stop through CueStackManager, exactly as a cue slot presses: a toggle, lit from the stack's activeCueId, live without being the playhead. The engine is untouched; the pinned pad's go-to-stack-at-cue call loses its only caller.

Solo

Every pad is a toggle, so solo has one meaning: pressing one turns its siblings off. A layer sibling is narrowed on the pressed heads — today's rule; a cue sibling is stopped. A cue press turns layer siblings off wholesale, because it has no targets. Off: nothing else moves.

The ring

Per record, as now. A record on two pads lights on both, and either pad turns it off. What differs between the two is only the on press's side effects: the bank decides what else goes off. Said in the bank header, not hidden.

Still there

/templates/{id}/toggle and /looks/{id}/toggle stay for the programmer's ⌥click strip and the AI, always siblingless. Long-press on a pad still opens the record's editor.

The busking-view decision, restated. "A busk pad presses a named thing from the library onto the selection" stands — every pad is still a template, a Look or a cue, and §4a's objection was to a second grid minting anonymous effects. What changes hands is who says where a pad sits and what else goes off when it is pressed: the operator, in the layout, instead of the library's family and group.
