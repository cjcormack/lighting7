Effect pads live in their family column, under a hairline. The column is the family; the hairline is the kind. Press puts a layer on the selection, press again takes it off — the same toggle a value pad already is, so a lit effect pad reads exactly like a lit colour pad, presence dot included. Long-press still opens the editor.

Beam has no effects, so its column has no hairline. The Looks pool is unchanged: a Look with deferred effects is still the multi-family or multi-effect chase, and it stays where it is. Tempo for a busked effect template follows its own master; the rail retunes it live.
