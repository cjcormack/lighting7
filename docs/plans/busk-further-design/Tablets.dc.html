The numbers are the app's existing ones: sm 640 (the sheet primitive's own), md 768 (rail, palette, edit), 600px of page body (column stacking), 500px of height (short: the cell editor's side sheet and the header's un-sticking), 750px of height (the colour editor's compact layout), 1100 and 800 (the selection bar's word and verb folds). No new breakpoint is introduced; the rig band and the side sheet ask the same questions the grid and the cell editor already ask, through useCellEditorForm and the container queries, so a surface can never get a form the rest of the app would not give it.
