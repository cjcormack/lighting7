Other ideas, and three things declined

Each is one card because each can be taken or left on its own. The tag says what it costs: cheap is a client afternoon on what the six boards already build, backend touches lighting7, bigger is a session of its own.

Target pads in a bank

MoversBars

A fourth pad kind, TARGET: a group or fixture placed in a bank, whose press is selection.toggle. It is what MagicQ's execute grid and Eos's direct selects do, and it is what makes a Pads-focus screen able to select at all. A solo bank of target pads is a radio selection — one group at a time — which is the busking ask nobody has phrased yet.

Special pads

MagicQ's Special Functions: Next page, Prev page, Clear selection, Release bank (take every sibling off), Blind. A fifth kind, SPECIAL(fn), so a page can carry its own navigation for a touch screen with no keyboard and a MIDI surface with no page buttons. The MIDI targets already exist for four of the five; the pad is a face on them.

Flash: hold is momentary

FU-BUSK-MOMENTARY, already recorded. A pad's momentary flag: press on pointer-down, release on pointer-up, drawn with a small lightning corner. Blinders and strobes are the whole case. The hook change lives in useLongPress.ts; the pad's hold menu moves to a right-click / two-finger hold on a momentary pad, since the hold is taken.

Hold a rig tile, drag for level

The speed card's hold-to-slide gesture, on intensity: hold a group tile and drag up or down to set its dimmer, programmer.set throttled through the same useLiveTempoPush. “Grab the front wash and pull it down” is the single most common busking hand movement and it has no gesture here. The tile's hold is free today — a target pad is a plain toggle.

Live colour on every tile

Drawn on the Main board already: a 3px bar under each tile, per cell on a multi-head, from the same colour-source dispatch the mini-stage uses. It turns the rig into a magic sheet for free — the operator sees what each group is doing without looking up — and it is what makes Pick on the Colour tab honest.

cheap

Plot: the rig as the patch drew it

Arrange: Plot on the Rig board. The same tiles, placed by stageX / stageY from the patch, scaled to the band; groups in a strip above since they have no position. Drag selection as a lasso over the plot, the marquee's own machinery. No coordinates of its own — move a light on the stage view and the plot follows.

Save selection as group

A group is a saved selection, so the band's label row gains Save as group… beside Clear: name it, and it appears in the Rig palette and can be dropped on a row. Titan's Quick Record for groups. Cells included once session 5 lands, which is how “odd cells of both bars” becomes one tile.

cheap

Next / Prev on the surface

The Cells chip's Next and Prev as MIDI buttons (SelectionNext / SelectionPrev) — MA's MAtricks stepping on a hardware button. With a template pad lit, stepping the selection and re-pressing is a manual chase; with Spread live, stepping walks the fan. The X-Touch's two encoder buttons are the obvious home.

The second colour hands over

On the Colour tab, Second colour, spread across the selection switches to the Spread tab with From set to the picked colour and To focused — one gesture from a colour to a fan. The reverse: Spread's From/To swatches open the picker inline. Two tabs, one picker component.

cheap

Focus from the hardware

BuskFocusSet and BuskSheetToggle as window-addressed MIDI targets: a binding names a window by its registry name, so one button on the desk flips Screen 2 between pads and rig. The Screens sheet's focus picker is the same command from the other end.

backend

Declined: a rig per page

A page-scoped rig would give the colour screen and the position screen two different selection surfaces for one selection, which is exactly the two-screen flow going wrong. One rig per project; a page that wants fewer targets is a row the operator scrolls to, or a target pad in one of its banks.

declined

Declined: free positioning and a second programmer

Free x/y on rig tiles repeats what busk-layout D2 rejected: a coordinate system nothing else reads. The plot uses the patch's. And a programmer per window (FU-PROG-PER-USER) stays out: the Colour and Spread tabs write to the one shared programmer, which is what lets Screen 1's picker and Screen 2's pads mean the same thing.
