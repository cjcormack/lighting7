Issue 4No field for a value. A slider and a read-out in bytes and percent. Only the Channels view takes a typed number — a raw channel.

3

Issue 6Effects come after every property, folded. On a 19-channel head that is below the fold of a 1000px window; on a 12-head bar it is above twelve accordions.

4

Pan and tilt write raw channels. The sheet's sliders go through the channel shim into the programmer's side track, which holds no effect back — while the provenance frame reports the programmer as the winner. The grid writes a position entry, which does.

5

Issue 1Nothing says who is driving a value. The sheet reads the output only. Park's lock icon is the one source it shows; programmer, effect and cue all look the same.

6

Aim is pinned to the foot in the Stage panel, so effects scroll under it. Below 640px the panel is not drawn at all.

!

Issue 1Why a Circle after a set position did nothing. The sheet's + makes a manual effect (priority 0). The engine skips every non-programmer effect on a property the programmer holds, so the Circle ran, held back, with nothing on screen saying so. And under the default Override it would have circled 128/128 anyway: only Additive orbits what is underneath.

!

Issue 2No clear. programmer.clearEntry exists and the grid's ⌫ uses it; the sheet never offers it.

!

Issue 3Every FX form is a draft. Apply / Update, then look. The desk's PUT /fx/{id} already swaps parameters keeping the phase.
