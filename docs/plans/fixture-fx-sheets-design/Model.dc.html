The wire
W1programmer.keyStack {targetType, targetKey, propertyName} → the layers under one property, top first: park · programmer effects · programmer slots (owner, value) · effects, each with heldBack · the cue's contributor (cue, stack, Look layer, value) · base. Request and reply on one socket, never broadcast; built from ProvenanceService.underlyingSources and the engine's suppression map. Re-asked while the stack is open on provenanceState. W2programmer.clearTarget {targetType, targetKey, fadeMs} — every entry on the fixture (its heads included) or the group, and every local effect on it, in one pass and one republish. Answers what it released, which Release's toast names. W3No new route for effects: POST /fx/add with programmerOwned: true; Around is blendMode: ADDITIVE plus the centre at 128. Live edits are PUT /fx/{id} at a 50 ms floor (open call 1). W4A bug to fix beside it: provenance counts the programmer's raw-channel side track as holding a property, though the engine holds no effect back for it, so a Channels-tab pan reads Programmer while an effect is what is on stage.

Open calls — each drawn one way
1

Live FX over REST or a socket frame? Drawn: PUT /fx/{id} through useLivePush at 50 ms — no new protocol, about 20 requests a second while dragging. The alternative is a WS fx.update beside pauseFx.

2

A level effect over a held level: Replace or Within? Drawn as a proposal on Pulse — Within it is Multiply, pulsing between 0 and your 80%. Leaving it out keeps today's Override.

3

Release asks nothing. Drawn without a confirm, like the programmer's Clear: it fades at the programmer fade and the toast names what went.

4

The strip's pick is the sheet's own, not the desk selection; it resets when the sheet opens on another fixture.

5

A row's editor opens inline (colour, position) rather than as a popover beside the sheet; one open at a time.
