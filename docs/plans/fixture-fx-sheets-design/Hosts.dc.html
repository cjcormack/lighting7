1
Width is the only ladder.

At 512 everything has its word; at 380 Release folds to its glyph and the scope line drops · fade; on a phone the rows take finger sizes. The rows are a container query on the sheet, never the viewport, so a pop-up and a panel of one width draw the same.

2
A row's editor opens in place.

Colour and Position have a face and a chevron; one row is open at a time, so the sheet never stacks two pickers. A setting row's list and a row's stack open through EditorSurface — beside the row on a desk, a bottom sheet on a phone.

3
The overview panel and Channels open the pop-up as they do today; the Groups route opens the group sheet (Heads and groups board). There is no read-only mode to forward any more.

4
Channels stays raw.

The Channels tab is today's channel list, with a field per channel — it writes raw channels, which the desk lifts into the programmer, and says so in its scope line.

5
Focus tab only in the Stage view, as today: it needs the scene to land a beam on.
