Both lit at once. Ballyhoo and Downstage Centre are fighting over the same six pan/tilt channels, and neither group can say so: a template group only knows templates, a Look group only knows Looks. This is exactly the case in the brief.

For. The smallest change. No rule to reverse, nothing leaves its column, the Looks pool gains clusters and a server-owned order, and the template side is untouched.

Against. Two of everything: look_groups, a second whole-layout route, a second templateLayout.ts, a second family question — and the answer there is "no family rule for Look groups", so the two group kinds would carry different rules under one name.

Against, decisively. It cannot express "one movement at a time" once the movements are a palette and a pattern. The brief's example stays impossible, and the fix later is this canvas's recommendation anyway — with a migration on top.

Where it fits. Nowhere on its own. Its one real idea — clusters in the Looks pool with a dense order — is contained in the recommendation.
