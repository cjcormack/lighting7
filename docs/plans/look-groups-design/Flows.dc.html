One button turns the page editable in place. Bank names become fields, Solo becomes a switch, pads grow a cross, an empty slot at the end of every bank waits for a drop, and the page ends in + Bank. Each gesture saves; Done only leaves the mode.

The library sits where the speed rail was: templates, Looks and cues under one search and a kind filter, every row a pad. Drag a row onto a bank and the slot opens where it lands; drop, and it is placed. A record already on the page says on page and may be placed again.

Drag a pad within a bank or into another; the drop slot is a dashed gap, the source stays ghosted. On touch, long-press lifts — the same hook that opens the editor outside edit mode. A bank drags by its grip: beside a column makes a new column, under another bank stacks it, below the page starts a row. Pages drag by their tab. dnd-kit is already in the app.

Solo on. Press Downstage: Ballyhoo goes off on the pressed heads. Press Blackout: it comes on and Ballyhoo and Downstage go off everywhere. Press it again and it stops.
Solo off. Pads stack. Pressing one never touches another — the ungrouped behaviour of today, now a choice per bank rather than a consequence of the library.

The switch is the whole of exclusivity. In play mode it shows as a small solo tag in the bank header, so the operator can tell a radio bank from a stacking one before pressing.
