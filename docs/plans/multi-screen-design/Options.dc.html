Three ways to move a thing between windows

The ask was drag and drop; the honest answer is that a pointer drag cannot leave a browser window in general, so each option is a different way of making the move not depend on one.

A · The handrecommended
Pick up here, place there — a desk-owned held item

A hold on any record opens its menu with Pick up; every window draws the chip and lights its targets; a tap places. One mechanism for every pair of windows, and for touch on one.

ReachesDesk screens, iPad, phone, and a MIDI button (Pick up / Place as bindings)CostsTwo gestures where a drag is one. A new frame family and a new desk fact. The chip is one more thing on a live view — it must sit where the ShowBar's GO cannot be hidden by it.
B · Native drag, same browser
HTML5 drag-and-drop between two windows of one browser on one machine

The browser carries a dataTransfer across its own windows for free. A pad would have to be a native draggable as well as a dnd-kit one, and the drop resolved from the payload.

ReachesThe two desk screens onlyCostsNothing reaches the iPad. dnd-kit and native DnD on one element fight over the pointer; the ghost is the browser's, not ours. Feels great when it works and silently does nothing otherwise.
C · Send to…
A menu: send this record to a bank / slot / stack on a named window

The hold menu grows a Send to submenu listing every bank on every page, and the layer stack. No held state, no second window involved.

ReachesEverything, with no gesture at allCostsChoosing a destination from a list is the thing a drag exists to avoid, and a page with twelve banks makes a long menu. Right as the accessible fallback under A, wrong as the primary.
