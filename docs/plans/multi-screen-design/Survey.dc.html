1 · Screens are views on one programmer

Every console with a physical second screen treats it as another view on the same state, and every one lets you choose what each screen shows. None of them makes the second screen a second desk. Our windows already are views on one desk; what is missing is the naming and the show X on screen 2 gesture.

2 · A remote is either the same programmer or a second user

Eos and MagicQ: the same. MA and Titan: a second user with a programmer of their own, because their remotes exist for a second person. Chris's ask is one operator reaching the desk from an iPad, so follow by default, unlink per window covers it, and a second-user mode stays a later, separate desk fact.

3 · Nobody drags across screens

A console moves a window to a screen; it does not drag a palette from one screen onto another. The hand has no precedent among the desks — its precedents are the Move key (pick up, then place) and the mobile clipboard. The edge drag is the one thing here that is genuinely new, and it is a shortcut over the hand, never the only route.
